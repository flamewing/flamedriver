S3K <-> SC Differences
----------------------
Envelope 02 is shorter in SC (S3K has 18h, SC 07h bytes)
Envelope 03 is completely different

SC has only 0Ch Envelopes, S3K has 27h


S3K <-> S3D Differences
-----------------------
Envelope 04 is longer in S3D (S3K has 0Bh, S3D 23h bytes)
Envelope 26 is shorter in S3D (S3K has 64h, S3D 62h bytes)
S3D has an additional envelope 28


S1 <-> S2 Differences
---------------------
S2 has 3 additional envelopes (S1 has 0Ah, S2 has 0Dh envelopes)


S2 <-> S3K
----------
The envelopes are completely different, so they aren't compareable.
Here is a conversion table:
S2	S3K
01	0A (close, but shorter and with small fade-in)
02	02
03	0C?
04	27 (close)
05	1C (close)
06	26 (S3K one is longer)
07	1D (S3K one is longer)
08	1D (more is less close)
09	08/15 (close)
0A	-
0B	-
0C	-
