Sonic-series volume-envelope usage survey
=========================================

Scope and counting
------------------
Tone IDs are the one-based values used in song/SFX data. Tone 01 selects
pointer-table entry 00, tone 27 selects entry 26, and 00 means no envelope.
Each M/S cell counts distinct music/SFX sequence assets that select a tone
in a PSG header or PSG track. It does not count notes or loop iterations.
Both initial header tones and reachable in-track F5 tone changes count.
Explicitly marked Unreachable blocks were excluded. A song can appear in
several tone rows. A dash means the driver has no entry for that tone.

S1/S2 and S3/S&K: public Sonic Retro music and SFX disassemblies. The S3
and S&K columns include their respective named variants plus shared assets
from the combined disassembly. This is a driver asset catalog, not a claim
that every listed song is encountered in standalone gameplay.
S3D: original retail-song .s3k rips (28 listed tracks) and 169 SFX entries
decoded from the retail ROM using the public driver pointer table and
command lengths. The unreferenced 0Eb Gumball Machine rip is excluded.
SC: six Sonic and Crackers song rips and 16 SFX rip files. The research
pack notes that this prototype has broken SFX pointers; its SFX column
describes stored data, not confirmed audible playback.
Flamedriver is omitted because it is a cross-game port, not another game.

Observations
------------
* S1 uses all nine tones somewhere; tones 02 and 07 occur only in SFX.
* S2 tone 0D is defined but has no music or SFX selection in the surveyed
  assets. Tone 06 is SFX-only; the other defined tones are referenced.
* S3/S&K have many defined but unselected volume envelopes. The one
  S3-only tone in this catalog is 22, in a Sonic 3 variant song.
* S3D music favors tone 0F; S3D SFX mostly reuse S3/S&K tone choices,
  but S3D SFX 6C selects tone 0D whereas S3/S&K SFX 48 does.
* S3D tone 27 selects the addressable PSGTone_26 and appears in three
  songs. The unpointed PSGTone_27 is not selected by tone 27.
* SC music uses five of twelve tones; the stored SFX have one nonzero
  selection, tone 02 in SFX 21 (Act Clear SFX).
* No FM volume-envelope command was found in the surveyed S3/S&K song/SFX
  sources or the decoded S3D and SC sequences. The later drivers support
  FM volume envelopes, but this catalog does not select them for FM tracks.
